## **PQCP Update \- Matthias Kannwischer**

* Project Lifecycle Stage Review (see TSC issue [\#178](https://github.com/pq-code-package/tsc/issues/178))  
  * Self evaluation: Growth Stage (striving for Impact Stage soon)  
  * Rationale: mlkem-native is adopted by both AWS-LC and liboqs  
  * It makes little sense to evaluate PQCP as a whole;  
    Our interpretation: At least one sub-project is in Growth Stage  
* mlkem-native [release announcement Blog post](https://lists.pqca.org/g/content-review/message/5). Ready to publish?  
* Discussing common documentation standards for PQCP projects  
  (see TSC issue [\#151](https://github.com/pq-code-package/tsc/issues/151))  
* [mldsa-native](https://github.com/pq-code-package/mldsa-native): Major progress in CBMC proofs;   
  Top-level keygen and verification proven \- only missing: signing  
* [slhdsa-c](https://github.com/pq-code-package/slhdsa-c): Supports all 12 FIPS 205 parameter sets and \~300 experimental reduced parameter sets under discussion for standardization by NIST.
