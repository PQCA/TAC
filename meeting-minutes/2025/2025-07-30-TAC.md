## **CBOM kit tool discussions** 

Before the official meeting began, **Sandip Dholakia** and **Andreas Schade** had a brief discussion regarding the **CBOM Kit Tool**. They plan to continue these discussions separately.

There was also a conversation about the potential of making the CBOM Kit a formal project. **Nicklas Kortge** and **Andreas Schade** raised questions, and **Hart Montgomery** provided insights into the process. Montgomery proposed setting up a dedicated meeting to discuss this further.

---

## **PQCA \- Blogposts**

Two blog posts—Mlkem Native and CBOM Kit—were reviewed by both the TAC and the Content Review Committee. It was agreed that both would be published on the PQCA website and shared via the LinkedIn page.

Montgomery noted:

* The blog posts will be published one at a time, with a gap between them.

* Members interested in contributing articles or papers should reach out; PQCA is open to posting these on its social media platforms.

## **Other Agenda topics**  

* **Project Lifecycle – OQS**:  
  **Jarvis** mentioned the project lifecycle stage for OQS. **Ashley** noted that this topic came up during the **TSC** discussions, but no decision has been finalized yet. Jarvis asked whether the TSC might address this in their next meeting; Norman responded that it's a possibility.

* **Annual Review of Project Lifecycle Document**:  
   Jarvis requested a volunteer to lead the review of the project lifecycle document. **Ashley** volunteered and inquired about the timeframe. Jarvis suggested aiming for the end **of August**. Montgomery offered support and encouraged Ashley to reach out with any questions.

* **PQCA Website and Wiki Update**:  
   Jarvis brought up updates to the PQCA website and its linked wiki page. Montgomery recommended sending the request to **Balaji Ethirajulu** and himself for processing.

## **PQCP Update \- Matthias Kannwischer**

* Ml-kem our arm backend is merged into AWS LC two days ago and that is a great milestone.  If you need a faster ARM ml-kem, it is now in AWS LC  
* For ml-dsa native, we have completed the cvmc proofs.  
* Working on constant time hardening and we will be looking at performance to have the matching state of the art for SLH-DSA. There has been some work on integrating this into lib OQS.
