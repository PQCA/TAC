# Discussion & Updates

### **Introduction of 1st Time Attendees**

The TAC welcomed Biplab from AMD, who provided a brief introduction and shared their background with the group.

---

### **OQS Metrics and LFX Insights**

The team reviewed ongoing discussions around **OQS metrics** and the **LFX Insights dashboard**, focusing on data quality and relevance. [Concerns raised by the OQS project](https://github.com/open-quantum-safe/tsc/issues/216) regarding inaccurate metrics had been relayed to the LFX team, who responded with a remediation plan to improve data reliability.

Brian expressed appreciation for the responsiveness and emphasized the importance of providing reliable, “out-of-the-box” metrics for projects. He noted that the TAC will revisit LFX Insights progress and OQS-related concerns during the next meeting.

---
### **mldsa-native Blog Post Review and Publication Process**

Christina confirmed that the mldsa-native blog post draft is currently under review by the content review team and is on track for publication on Tuesday, November 25.

The TAC reaffirmed the standard one-week content review period before publication. Brian noted that the improved content review workflow has significantly streamlined the process compared to earlier practices.

---
### **Upcoming Meeting Schedule**

* **December 3**: Aditya will lead the meeting in Brian’s absence.

* **December 17**: The TAC will decide on December 3 whether to hold the December 17 meeting, given holiday schedules.

* **December 31**: The TAC agreed to cancel the December 31 meeting due to the holidays.

### **GitHub Open Issues Review**

The TAC reviewed [outstanding issues](https://github.com/PQCA/TAC/issues) from the previous retrospective:

* Brian agreed that the new issue-based meeting agenda process has improved efficiency and transparency.
* Brian plans to resume sending agendas in advance, following his Monday sync meetings with Aditya.
* The PR review timeline was discussed, but no new actions were assigned.
* The TAC held an in-depth discussion on developer education content during the 10/22 TAC meeting.
* Christina shared that the blog author spotlight feature is complete, with a new post from Matthias scheduled for release next Tuesday.
* Christina confirmed The content review process has been fully documented: Content review process added to the TAC repo; PQCA wiki updated with new blog submission guidelines; Submission form updated to reflect the new process.
* Several retrospective-related issues were closed; comments were added to the three remaining open issues for next steps.

Open follow-up items from those issues include:

* Hart will share a content outline for a new educational/training program.
* Hart suggested exploring LLM-assisted social media post generation, with staged review before publication.
* The TAC continues to explore increased Discord usage for community engagement.
