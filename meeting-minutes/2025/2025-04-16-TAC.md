## Retrospective - Michael Maximilien
* For retrospective discussions, Max used Easy Retro application/dashboard. He created three categories, namely - 1)what went well, 2) what went ok, 3)What to improve and then based on that do the action. Then members can vote and decide on which items to discuss and act upon.


* Members wrote several topics and voted on it. Some of the key areas that came up for discussions and improvement are: increase contributors/maintainers, more company involvement in terms of contributions, blogs, social media promotions.


* Yarkin also suggested, many of these topics go under marketing and communications. Ethirajulu suggested that a new outreach committee has been created and they can help in all marketing and other promotional activities. Hart Montgomerry also suggested that social media activities and promotions are topic for outreach committee discussions and encouraged the TAC members to join outreach committee meeting.
